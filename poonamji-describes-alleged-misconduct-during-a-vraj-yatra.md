## Summary

In this video, the speaker alleges that on a Vraj yatra about 15 years ago a Bawashri tried to hug her, that his followers pressed her to accept, and that London devotees also take part.

* The speaker alleges that a Bawashri made gestures to hug her, and that when she refused and offered only charan sparsh he insulted her and told her to leave [01:01]
* The speaker alleges that the sakhis, the men around him, and Mukhiyaji all pressed her, saying she was making a mistake because Bawashri himself had asked for her [01:28]
* The speaker alleges that the sakhis who stayed near him would go to bathe him, and that her brother's wife said he does not bathe himself [02:17]
* The speaker alleges that a woman from Ahmedabad had waited four days and asked that Bawashri take her turn quickly, which Mukhiyaji called a laabh [02:47]
* The speaker says London people and other foreigners also know about these practices and take part, and arrange long stays with Bawashri in Vraj [03:56]
* The speaker alleges that a man from the Haveli told her that in Junagadh a father leaves his own daughter with Babashri for the whole night [04:38]
* The speaker alleges that the women who pressed her said they do send their own daughters in law and daughters to Bawashri [05:12]
