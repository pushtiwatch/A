## Summary

In this video, the speaker asks Vaishnavs not to question women who speak about angikar, and alleges that haveli associates and even her own guru use women.

* The speaker alleges that at two different havelis, people close to the Baba said they would do her two and a half year old daughter's Naam Nivedan only if she took Brahma Sambandh again [01:48]
* The speaker says that men have no right to question a woman who comes forward about what happened to her, and that they should search nearby havelis themselves [02:51]
* The speaker alleges that people who stay close to the Bawas each have several affairs [03:55]
* The speaker says it is more necessary to stay away from those who deny that the angikar practice exists and who say the Bawa is Vallabh himself [05:03]
* The speaker says she has seen Vaishnavs who visit the haveli weekly being slowly lured in, and that people who receive Ashishbhai's videos dismiss them as a movie [05:50]
* The speaker alleges that the guru who gave her the Brahma Sambandh mantra also has the habit of using women [06:45]
