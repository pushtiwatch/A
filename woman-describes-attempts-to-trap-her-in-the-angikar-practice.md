## Summary

In this video, the speaker alleges that a Goswami Balak tried to pull her into angikar leela, came to her house at night, and that she saw the same practice on yatra and in other homes.

* The speaker alleges that a Goswami Balak mentally tortured her because she was not ready to take part in his angikar leela, and that she even had thoughts of suicide [00:30]
* The speaker alleges that at one o'clock at night he came to her house, hugged her and kissed her forehead, and that she told him she would not see the morning sun if she fell into that leela [04:23]
* The speaker alleges that on the yatra, ladies dressed up and went turn by turn into the pals at night to be with JJ [07:02]
* The speaker alleges that on Sharad Manorath a Vaishnav tried to take her for angikar in Vrajbhoomi, and that she was saved when another Vaishnav took her back [08:06]
* The speaker alleges that when he wants to do angikar at a Vaishnav's house he first calls the husband to confirm he is away, and targets women who live without a mother in law [12:33]
* The speaker alleges that a Vaishnav in Sion invited her to a supposed Manorath, took her into a decorated bedroom, and said she wanted JJ to do the speaker's angikar there [21:53]
* The speaker alleges that in Nathdwara JJ asked whether her complete angikar should happen there, and that she refused [28:32]
