## Summary

In this video, the speaker alleges that women in her mother's Vaishnav satsang circle in Bombay engage in intimate physical conduct and claim divine encounters with Thakorji, and argues that this defames the marg.

* The speaker alleges that she saw her mother and another member of the satsang feed each other lip to lip, after the group hugged and kissed each other and called each other gopi [04:47]
* The speaker says her mother told her that questioning these practices would bring aparadh (sin) [05:37]
* The speaker alleges that members at a gathering described Thakorji drinking their milk and doing leela with them at night [06:34]
* The speaker alleges that Vaishnavs perform angikar on each other, before Bavashris do, and says she saw this with her own eyes [07:42]
* The speaker alleges that a guest who stayed overnight after a path at her family's Bombay flat made sounds in the night and later said Thakorji was doing leela with her [09:39]
* The speaker alleges that the women touch each other intimately, claiming that Thakorji touched them there, and asks where this is written in the Vaishnav marg [12:06]
* The speaker alleges that roaming brokers of Bavashri and some Shastrijis who do saptahs from house to house eye other people's wives and daughters, and says she will expose them in later recordings [13:16]
