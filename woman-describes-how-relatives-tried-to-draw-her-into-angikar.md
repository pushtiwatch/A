## Summary

In this video, the speaker alleges that relatives and a man who also used the name Tushar tried to draw her into angikar, first among Vaishnavs and then with a Bavashri in Kandivali.

* The speaker alleges that Manishaben brought a Vaishnav from Vadodara called Manishbhai to the speaker's house at night, saying Thakorji had given him prerna to come [06:09]
* The speaker alleges that the next morning Manisha said Thakorji came to Manishbhai in a dream and told him he must do the speaker's uddhar [07:21]
* The speaker alleges that Manishbhai, whose real name he said is Tushar, told her to stop her law studies, keep distance from her husband, and sleep in a separate room [22:23]
* The speaker alleges that when she was alone at home he explained the meanings of seva offerings in obscene language and called it an advanced stage of leela [27:24]
* The speaker alleges that Manisha said a Bavashri in Kandivali did her angikar, and that he does angikar with a woman named Rashmi whenever he visits her house [30:40]
* The speaker says her mother scolded them only for speaking too soon, and that a Betiji told her to block them and stay out of such satsang groups [33:17]
* The speaker alleges the plan was that she would first do angikar with Tusharbhai and then be taken to that Bavashri in Kandivali [38:34]
