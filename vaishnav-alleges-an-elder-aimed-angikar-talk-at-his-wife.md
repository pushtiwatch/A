## Summary

In this video, the speaker alleges that an elderly Vaishnav turned a health visit into talk of angikar aimed at the speaker's wife, and urges support for those who speak against the practice.

* The speaker says that after about one and a half to two years of marriage, an elderly Vaishnav known in the village would come to the shop and do satsang of Mahaprabhuji, Gosaiji, and the 84 and 252 [00:53]
* The speaker alleges that after they visited him following an operation, he shifted the talk to angikar and said the tilak Thakorji wears is of a woman's private parts [01:33]
* The speaker alleges that the man said Baba had not partaken of beautiful samagri for a long time, speaking with intent toward the speaker's wife [01:49]
* The speaker says that about two days later he heard Purushottam Bava of Junagadh explain Harirai Mahaprabhu's Kamakhya Dosh, and then went to Junagadh to meet him [02:22]
* The speaker says Vaishnavs should do seva at home and read Mahaprabhuji, Harirai Mahaprabhu's 84 and 252, Shikshapatra, Dayarambhai's Bhakti Poshan, and Vallabhakhyan [04:04]
* The speaker alleges that Ashishbhai keeps speaking on YouTube to protect the marg even after receiving threats to kill him [04:41]
* The speaker says Vaishnavs should support Ashishbhai so the coming generation can know the true siddhants of Mahaprabhu [05:16]
