## Summary

In this video, the speaker explains that after a Balak came to her house at night she stopped going upstairs in the haveli, and went on Vraj yatra only with elders who stayed with her.

* The speaker says that after JJ came to her house at night she stopped going upstairs in the haveli, and made excuses when Vahuji or Bhabhiji called [00:33]
* The speaker says she still went downstairs at the haveli because she stitches vastra and does Pichwai painting, work that supports her household [01:44]
* The speaker says the first experience was with the Balak, and the second was when a Vaishnav called her to a house for a Manorath, which is how she learned of dalal Vaishnavs [02:35]
* The speaker says that after JJ came at night she could not sleep and would cry during seva, asking what mistake she had made [03:25]
* The speaker says she went on Vraj yatra because elders in her pal knew her situation and promised to stay with her [04:43]
* The speaker alleges that on the yatra a sister tried to take her during Sharad's Manorath, which she found to be torture [07:56]
* The speaker says she offers only from her own satta, and refused other Vaishnavs stocking her grains because that is against Mahaprabhuji's command [08:31]
