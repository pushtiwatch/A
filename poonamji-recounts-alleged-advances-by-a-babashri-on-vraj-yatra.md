## Summary

In this video, the speaker alleges that during a Vraj yatra about 15 years ago Babashri tried to hug her, that she was pressed to accept, and that even London devotees take part.

* The speaker alleges that a Babashri made gestures to hug her, and that when she refused and offered only charan sparsh he insulted her and told her to leave [01:01]

* The speaker alleges that the sakhis who stayed around him would go to bathe him, and that her brother's wife said he does not bathe on his own [02:17]
* The speaker alleges that a woman from Ahmedabad had waited four days and asked that Bawashri take her turn soon, which Mukhiyaji called a Laabh [02:48]
* The speaker says London people also know about these practices and take part, and arrange to stay with Bawashri for 15 days at a stretch in Vraj [04:13]
* The speaker alleges that a man from the Haveli told her that in Junagadh a father leaves his own daughter with Babashri for the whole night [04:41]
* The speaker alleges that the women who pressed her said they do send their own daughters in law and daughters with Bawashri [05:11]
